Good to know

  • Please arrive 10 minutes early to settle in and pick your project. We're in a plaza with plenty of free parking, so getting here is easy.
  • Our experience is perfect for ages 4 and up.
  • There's no dress code, but we recommend skipping long sleeves. Aprons are provided.
  • You pick and pay for your project at the studio, from $50 per guest. We're a cashless studio, so please bring a card or contactless payment.
  • It's one project per guest. So everyone gets the full experience and our team can give the best service, two guests can't work on one project together.
  • Each table has room for up to four guests, so larger groups may be split across a couple of tables. You'll still be right next to each other.
  • Our studio is reserved for guests who are creating. This keeps it comfortable for everyone and gives our team room to guide you, so friends and family coming along are welcome to wait in our lobby. Kids who are creating can have one adult with them if needed.
  • For everyone's comfort and safety, we're not able to accommodate infants or strollers in the studio.
  • We have a menu of wines by the glass or bottle, beer and soft drinks you can enjoy during your experience. You're welcome to bring your own bottle of wine for a $30 corkage fee per bottle. Liquor isn't allowed.
  • Due to the hands-on nature of the experience and to protect your finished artwork, food isn't allowed in the studio.
  • Groups of six or more have an 18% gratuity added automatically, even if booked separately.
  • Your finished piece is ready in about 7 days. Pick it up at the studio (Wed to Sun, 11 AM to 6 PM), or we'll ship it anywhere in the contiguous U.S. for $25 per piece.

Need to make a change?

You can reschedule, change your party size or cancel for free up to 24 hours before your class, using the booking link in your confirmation text or email. After that, changes, cancellations and no-shows have a $50 per guest late-change fee.
